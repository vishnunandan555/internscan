## Core Contributions

InternScan thrives through collective effort across several engineering domains:

- **Modular ATS Adapters:** Authors of high-reliability scrapers for Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Meta GraphQL, Amazon Jobs, Google Careers, and Apple.
- **Classification Engine:** Contributors who refine domain keyword categorizers (`Software`, `AI/ML`, `Hardware/Silicon`, `Security`, `Backend/Infra`) and regional city hub matching across India, the US, Canada, the UK, and Europe.
- **Frontend & Design System:** Creators of the high-speed vanilla CSS design system, dark/light theme tokens, and dynamic client-side filtering.
- **Bot & Developer Tooling:** Contributors who built the low-memory (< 35 MB) Telegram broadcast bot and public REST/RSS APIs.
