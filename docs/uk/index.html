About InternScan UK

Direct Official Careers APIs

No stale job boards, aggregators, or expired postings. Every position is fetched directly from official employer ATS APIs (Workday, Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, Lever, Oracle HCM, Eightfold) every 3 hours.

Focused on UK Tech Hubs

Specifically filtered and categorized for engineering students and graduates seeking roles in London, Cambridge, Oxford, Manchester, Edinburgh, and Remote UK.

Automated Lifecycle & Zero Fluff

When an employer fills or pulls a listing from their portal, it is automatically removed from this tracker during the next cycle, saving you from applying to ghost jobs.

100% Free & Open Source

Operated via GitHub Actions. Developers and students can submit new company adapters or improvements directly on the GitHub repository.
